05. Projects

Featured project

🍃 Leaf Recognition

An end-to-end computer-vision system that identifies plant species from a photo of a single leaf — and reports the scientific name. It segments the leaf with OpenCV, extracts 20 hand-crafted shape, colour and texture features, and classifies with a scikit-learn SVM. An optional MobileNetV2 transfer-learning backend boosts real-world accuracy. Shipped three ways: a CLI, a Tkinter desktop app, and an installable mobile web app (FastAPI + PWA), with a full pytest suite.
